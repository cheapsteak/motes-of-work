Per-part settle windows

A mechanism part can set `settle: [from, to]` to choose the assembly window in
which its motes land; omitted keeps the role default. Role defaults live in one
ROLE_SETTLE table shared by settleWindow() and the contract test, which checks
that any declared window is non-empty and inside the default for its role.
