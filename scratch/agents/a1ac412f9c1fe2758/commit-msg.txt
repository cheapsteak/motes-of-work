feat: surface normals, lighting and depth cues

Thread unit normals (correct and twin) from the shape sampler through
BoundPoint, MoteAttrs and the instanced layout into MoteOut.normal, with an
identical TS/GLSL fallback when the variant blend passes through zero. The
GLSL MoteOut struct is generated from PARITY_FIELDS; parity gains an
opposite-normals case and runs its hash check in the vertex stage.

Settled motes are lit (fill + key + rim, back-face and depth dimming, depth
size shrink) from constants in src/scene/lighting.ts passed as uniforms; the
depth range is the camera to the machine's anchor sphere per frame. Adds a
gear meshing regression test, a looks test for lit vs turned-away faces and
SHOTS-gated three-quarter mechanism screenshots.
