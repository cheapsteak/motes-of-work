mechanisms: a part may close its offset on its own arrival curve

By default a part closes its exploded offset on the motes' smoothstep
over its settle window. A part can now give an arrival(x) curve instead,
0 at the start of its window and 1 at the end, free to hold, fall and
rebound in between, so an entrance can have some character while staying
a pure function of assembly. The contract test bounds any such curve;
the monotone check applies to the default curve only.
