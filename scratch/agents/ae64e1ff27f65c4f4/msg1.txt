node: the network's position library, its TypeScript mirror and its motes

node.glsl and node.ts, step for step: a mote lands on its clock (builders on net.growth inside
their stack's duration, cube by cube; person motes on net.fix), flies in along its stream's Bezier
or falls from above, flares as it lands, carries the crack until the fix closes it, and lights by
the highlight and the fan-out surge. The surge's hop is clamped at 6 and both lights go out as the
fix lands. The constants live once in birth.ts and are spliced into the GLSL; NodeOut is generated
from the parity field list, NodeAttrs from nodeLayout.ts, and the two static textures are packed
by nodeData.ts for both mirrors. The motes themselves (nodeAttrs.ts) are surface points on every
stack's boxes, with the crack's twin on each cracked stack's crack cube (crack.ts) and its person
set; the first stack has none, its motes are the bike's. Three salts for where a mote sets out.
