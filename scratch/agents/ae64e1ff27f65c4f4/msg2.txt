node: the network drawn, with its streams, halos, the reviewer's ring and the feed

nodeSystem.ts builds five meshes over the two textures, once per page load: the stacks' motes
(node() in node.vert.glsl, lit and sized as the bike's motes are), the streams' drifting dots
(stream.vert.glsl, moving only with the scroll clock; the up-stream thin and brighter), the feed
to the ring (the same instance kind), one halo per stack (the far-glow impostor plus the highlight
and the surge) and the ring, placed by ringAt. Each frame reads the six network scalars, clock and
the camera; the whole group is hidden while net.show is 0, so no earlier frame changes.
mote.frag.glsl mixes a lit mote toward ember by a vHi varying the bike writes as 0. A hero whose
glow is under 1 is brought up to a plain mote's brightness as it grows past 120 to 300 px on
screen, so the close shots show its crack; the far shots keep the designed glow.

main.ts generates the network once, from the mote seed at the network's share of the mote budget,
and draws it beside the bike; the far plane moves out to hold the wide shots; drawStats reports
each visible instanced mesh's instance count.
