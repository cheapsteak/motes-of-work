bike: the frame is laid in place, tube by tube

FRAME_LAY (shapes.ts) lists the frame's tubes in a frame builder's order:
the bottom-bracket shell, the down tube, the head tube, the seat tube
with its collar, the top tube, the chainstays with the dropouts, the seat
stays, the brake bridge, and the cables last. Each tube has a slot of the
frame's settle window [0.81, 0.95]. It is built from the same named solids
as frame(), which now builds from that list in the same order; the frame's
points are unchanged.

The frame's settle field puts each mote in the slot of the tube whose
surface it lies on, 80 % by how far along the tube it is and 20 % by its
hash, so every tube is laid root first behind a soft front. The twin's
points map one to one, so its mast rises with the head tube and its
climbing top tube follows the correct one, with no rule of its own.

The frame no longer slides in: its exploded offset is the identity.
