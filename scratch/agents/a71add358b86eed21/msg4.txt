story: "Done" plays over one lap round the riding bike

vb-4 is one lap of the camera round the bike, from S1' back to S1', with
the bearing always rising: a low wide pass along the drive side, a crane
up behind looking down the bike's length, a high telephoto diagram from
the far side, a dive over the front onto the saddle on its mast (framed
close), then out across the front to rest. The bike rides meanwhile.

The lap is generated, not hand-keyed: orbitKeys() in shots.ts runs a
centripetal Catmull-Rom spline through seven waypoints in bearing,
elevation, ln distance, fov and target, against one inOutCubic over the
lap, so it leaves the hold and returns to it at zero speed. It keys every
1/36 of the lap and each waypoint; the keys are pasted into the keyframes
and a test regenerates them and holds the file to them. Other tests read
back each waypoint at its local, the start and end as S1', one rising
turn of 360 degrees, and the twin under the text band all the way round.

Two waypoints moved out to keep the text clear on a wide screen: the
crane to 5.2 m at fov 38, and the diagram to 6.3 m, still further and
longer-lensed than the crane. The dive's lens shift starts at the diagram,
0.006 earlier than designed, for the same reason.

The named frames of the cold open join the screenshot run.
