auto-advance: one stop per scene, at its end

A scene is a beat, or one line of the brief, with the motion that goes
with it. Its stop is just before the next change of text (the next line
coming up, or its page turning), so the motion has run and the line is
still fully up. Where the motion outlasts the line (the orbit), the stop
waits for it, at the latest point at rest before the next line. The
essay's last scene runs to the end. From the top of the essay it goes
straight on to the first stop rather than holding twice.
