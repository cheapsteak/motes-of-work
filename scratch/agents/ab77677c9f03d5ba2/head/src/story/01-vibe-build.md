You type one line, something like *make it handle the backlog*, and press enter.

The swarm floods in. Parts find each other, gears mesh, and in minutes a polished machine is turning. It looks finished. It looks impressive.

It is the wrong machine.

Not broken. Wrong. Every part was placed by an agent guessing at what you meant. Look at the core: nothing warm in it. No person ever touched the part that matters most.
