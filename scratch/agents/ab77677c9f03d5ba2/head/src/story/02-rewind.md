Scroll back. The machine runs in reverse, part by part, until it is a cloud again.

The effort does not come back with it.

Vibing the spec doesn't skip the spec. It defers it: until after you have paid for a confident, polished build of the wrong thing, and have to work out from the wreckage what you wanted all along.
