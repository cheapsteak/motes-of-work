scene: mech.steer, mech.ghostVariant and camera.frame

Three new SceneState scalars from the storyboard, each 0 by default and
each leaving every existing pose unchanged at 0:

- mech.steer adds to the drive's own steering as a fraction of full lock,
  clamped at the lock; pose takes it as an optional fourth argument, which
  machines that do not steer ignore.
- mech.ghostVariant is the variant the ghost outline is drawn in: its part
  texture and its points take the twin positions as the motes do.
- camera.frame composes the shot round the text at the top: the rig lands
  the authored target low-centre (wide) or lower, and right of the column
  on a wide screen (close). The portrait rig now keeps the authored target
  and no longer lifts the machine; the option is renamed compose. Without
  it the camera is exactly the plain camera.

The comparison bench gains steer and ghost-twin sliders. The e2e check that
the phone draws the machine in the upper frame is retired with the lift.
