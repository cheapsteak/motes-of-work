scene: ink on a sheet of paper, and the cloud dimmed behind it

The ink is one instanced draw of screen-space quads, one per segment
(triangles; nothing in it is a point). Its vertex shader poses each
segment with its part on the correct build or the twin's, presses it
toward the page by ink.flat (rear parts first), reveals it along the
pen's arc or the redraw's, fades struck-out lines to a pentimento, and
gives the pen a wobble and a pressure keyed on the stroke, so a line
never shivers between frames. The fragment shader draws a capsule with
round ends at a stroke's own ends, butt joins between its segments and
a nib where the pen has got to. Colours run from sepia on the page to
pale and warm on the stage as the sheet fades.

The sheet is a static quad of procedural paper (grain, fibres, flecks,
a vignette, a cut edge) that writes depth only while fully opaque. The
mote and thread draws take stage.motes as a brightness (uStage); the
mote() library and its parity are untouched.

SceneState gains eight leaves (sheet.opacity, ink.opacity, drawn,
redraw, flat, ring, tick, stage.motes) whose defaults leave every
existing frame as it was. inkLayout.ts builds the instances and
inkPose.ts mirrors the shader's posing and visibility on the CPU for
tests. The test hook reports what the last frame drew (drawStats).
