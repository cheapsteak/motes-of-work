e2e: the drawing is ink on paper, redrawn and lifted without a jump

drawing.spec.ts, at both sizes, canvas only, with regions projected
through the page's own camera:
- no gl.POINTS draws, the ink and the sheet on and the dotted ghost
  off, no blue-dominant pixel on the page, and the ink-dark pixels on
  the page rising through the pen's draw-in;
- the paper's margin within dE 6 of #E7D9B9, the ring's pixels ochre,
  and the lifted lines warm and pale on the stage colour;
- over 24 samples of the redraw, no more than 2.5% of the frame changes
  between samples, the ink never falls below 70% of what it was, and
  nothing changes more than 8 px from a moving stroke or the ring;
- the lift continuous (at most 4% between samples 0.002 apart, from
  where the camera's turn comes to rest).

The visible machine (e2e/machine.ts) counts the ink's visible strokes,
posed and pressed as the shader does, and the sheet's edge, so the
clearance test keeps the drawing and the page under the text. The
purity test gains four spec-section samples; the app test reads the
built bike at spec@0.95; the screenshot run names the spec section's
frames (shots/sp-*.png).
