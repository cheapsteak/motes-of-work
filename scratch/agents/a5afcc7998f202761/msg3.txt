bike: the bicycle drawn in ink, stroke by stroke

ink.ts builds the bike as a side elevation in polylines, from the same
constants and helpers as the solids: a tube is its two contour lines, a
wheel its tyre and rim circles, hub, spokes and reflector, a gear its
tooth outline, the chain a dashed line of links. Both builds are drawn
by the same calls, point for point, and a stroke moves (struck out and
redrawn) when its two builds stand apart once posed at rest: exactly
the saddle, the bars, and of the frame the seat tube, top tube, head
tube and cables. Page strokes (construction lines, hatching, three
blocks of mirror-wise scribble, the person's ring and tick) sit on the
ground part. The strokes come in the pen's order, under 3,900 segments
with the moving ones counted twice (the budget is 6,000).

The mechanism contract gains an optional ink() and sheet; the bike has
both. shapes.ts exports the few internals the builder reuses; a check
against the old file found its geometry unchanged.

Tests: determinism, point-for-point builds, the 30 mm segment limit and
the budget, the moving set against the posed builds, every bike stroke
within 15 mm of its own part's sampled surface on both builds, the pen
order and the redraw order, and the notes as texture (no glyph table,
no two rows alike).
