It runs. Push the pedals, and the back wheel goes round. Turn the front wheel, and it steers.

<!-- beat id=rv-2 at=0.175 -->
Now try to ride it.

<!-- page -->
<!-- beat id=rv-3 at=0.325 -->
The seat is on top of the steering post, over the front wheel. When the wheel turns, the seat turns with it.

<!-- beat id=rv-4 at=0.5125 -->
The handlebars are at the back, where the seat should be. They never turn.

<!-- page -->
<!-- beat id=rv-5 at=0.625 -->
You cannot sit up there and hold these down here. The frame is built around the mistake: to fix it, you need a new frame.

<!-- page -->
<!-- beat id=rv-7 at=0.825 -->
Every line on the list is met. Nobody wrote down that you sit on the seat and hold the bars. Who would?
