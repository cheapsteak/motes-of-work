## B. The mannequin (spec 4 I.1b, 5.8 Staging)
- What: a schematic, faceless "artist's wooden mannequin" of capsules/tubes (head, neck, torso, pelvis, upper arms, forearms, hands, thighs, shanks, feet), made of sparse, dim, neutral grey motes — never warm, never the agent blue (it is a user, not an author). It appears only in the reveal: fade in over roughly reveal@0.15..0.3, hold, fade out by reveal@0.9 so it is gone before the rewind. Every frame stays a pure function of scroll.
- Pose: an ordinary upright town-bike riding pose anchored on the twin's saddle (which sits on the steering column over the front wheel). Its hands close on empty air where the handlebars would be relative to a rider on that saddle (use the correct bike's saddle-to-grip offset), and its feet hang well ahead of the pedals (use the correct bike's saddle-to-pedal offset). The point: nothing it reaches for is there. It does not pedal. It turns with the saddle (the saddle steers).
- Proportions (fractions of height H; Winter, Biomechanics and Motor Control of Human Movement, fig. 4.1 after Drillis and Contini 1966): head 0.130; shoulder height 0.818; shoulder width 0.259; chest width 0.174; upper arm 0.186; forearm 0.146; hand 0.108; hip joint height 0.530; hip width 0.191; thigh about 0.245; shank about 0.246; ankle height 0.039; foot length 0.152. Riding-posture cross-check (Wang and Peng 2023, city e-bikes): hip-to-shoulder about 0.313 H seated, shoulder-to-wrist about 0.318 H. Use H = 1.75 m. Angles (weakly sourced; judge by eye): torso 65-80 deg from horizontal, hip about 100-120 deg, elbows 15-30 deg flexion, knee such that the pose reads seated. Credit Winter / Drillis and Contini in CREDITS.md under "Facts and data".
- How: do NOT change mote.glsl or mote.ts (parity). Render the mannequin as its own point set — for example reusing the ghost's point pipeline (a separate material instance with a neutral grey colour and its own opacity uniform) or a second small point system — driven by a new SceneState scalar (e.g. overlay.rider, 0..1, keyed only in the reveal). Add the leaf to defaultSceneState and the leaf list (a test keeps them in sync). Choose the simplest approach that keeps purity, then explain it in the report.
- Tests: proportions match the table (to 1e-6 of the constants); the hip sits on the saddle top within 0.03 m; each hand is within 0.05 m of the correct-bike grip position relative to the saddle and at least 0.3 m from the twin's actual handlebars; each foot is at least 0.2 m from every pedal at all drives in the reveal; the colour is neutral grey (no warm or blue hue: |r-g|,|g-b| small); overlay.rider is 0 outside the reveal (sample the shipped keyframes across the whole story) and reaches 1 inside it; jump-vs-scroll purity at a reveal progress includes the mannequin.
