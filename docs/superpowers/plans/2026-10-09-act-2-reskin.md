# Act II re-skin: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make Act II's three kinds of body read as different things:

- the centre becomes one black hole made of all the rules;
- plans become self-lit stars (dots and spikes) inside a schematic of their plan: R up close, H in the
  neighbourhood (7b);
- code becomes planets shaded by their own star;
- the spiral becomes the tuner's `RECOMMENDED` galaxy (1b), then its "Wisps (reviewed)" with the
  neighbourhood dissolving into it from afar (1c).

All of this stays inside the galaxy's budget, which the author made a provisional allowance of 93,500 instances (spec §7).

**Architecture:** The order is fixed by the budget, and the spec (§11 item 1) gives the reasons:

1. **1a**, the background's sprites and the haze, is first. It is self-contained and moves no point.
2. The stars come next. They shrink from lattices to dots and spikes, which frees the room the hole
   needs, and that room is reserved there.
3. The raised budget is paid for before the hole is drawn, by cutting vertex work. Hidden dots skip
   the heavy vertex path, which is pixel-identical. Every dot stays a 4-vertex quad: quads stay (the controller's ruling, 2026-10-10): the stage draws with 4× multisampling and the rasteriser snaps vertices to 1/16 px, so a triangle covers different samples from a square and moves a dot's edge pixels by up to 122/255, far beyond the 3/255 rounding bound.
4. **1b** then ports the tuner's `RECOMMENDED` preset into the generator. It comes after Task 3, so
   its stills become the new baseline the later tasks compare against.
   It keeps the number "1b": it is the second half of the background.
5. **1c** replaces it with the author's later pick: the wisps background, and the neighbourhood's 30
   stars dissolving into it at the wides (a level of detail, not a move).
   **1d** then widens the neighbourhood: systems at least 1.5 × their outer radii apart.
   **1e** makes a few close filler pairs S-type binaries that turn round their barycentres.
6. The hole is then built as a pure mirror pair (TS twins, GLSL, a `?lib=hole` parity mode) with no
   app change.
7. It is drawn in place of the six core bodies, which stop drawing.
8. The story's leaves then key it.
9. The planets get their terminator.
10. **7b** draws each plan's schematic round its star: R up close, H in the neighbourhood, handing over by
    projected size.
11. The freeze frame is brought to its mock.
12. **8b** gives the defect its motion: the scatter opens wounds that leak, and dark lines cross the
    piece.
13. The last task works through every `→ re-skin` backlog item and makes the contact sheet.

Every frame stays a pure function of (scroll, ambient).

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, three.js 0.186 (RawShaderMaterial, instanced dots as 4-vertex quads, GLSL3), Vite, Vitest
(Node), Playwright 1.56 on SwiftShader, pngjs.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-09-act-2-reskin.md`. Read it whole before your task. Its §4 holds every
look value, §5 the keys, §6 the beats, §7 the budget, §8 the shots and §10 the copy. Look references:

- `scratch/session/bhmock/blackhole.html`: the inspector. Its GLSL is the reference implementation.
- `scratch/session/galaxytuner/gen.js`: the tuner's generator.
- `scratch/session/lookref-findings.md`: the look research.

**Base:** `main` after the backlog sweep has landed and been pushed. The sweep moved the clearance
twins into `src/scene/node/galaxyOnScreen.ts`. Where this plan names a twin, use the name the sweep
left in that file.

  **The model, step by step (`mock.html`, `buildModel` and `buildS`):**
  1. **Pre-binary packing.** Run `placeStars` once at today's pair rule (`HOOD_SPACING` 1: outer_i +
     outer_j + `HOOD_MARGIN`), as the mock's option (a). At 1d's 1.5× no pair can be nearer than 1.35×
     its combined radii, so the close pairs must come from this packing.
  2. **Close pairs.** Take mutual nearest neighbours among the filler stars nearer than `BIN.pairK` ×
     their combined outer radii. Go through them closest first (by that ratio). Keep each when
     `streamFor(seed, 'binaries', pairKey)()` < `BIN.share`, at most `BIN.max`.
     - A scripted star is never in a pair, and above all N0's star (P_a): `bodyWorld` anchors the
       galaxy at N0, so a moving P_a would shake everything.
  3. **Each pair, S-type.**
     - The heavier star (by `starMass` of its brightness) is A.
     - e is drawn from `BIN.e`, with ω and phase from the pair's stream.
     - a_B is the pre-binary distance clamped to `BIN.a`.
     - Each star's limit is a_crit = `hwS`(m_other / M, e) · a_B.
  4. **Refit each star** with `fitSystem`:
     - a_crit is its bound in place of `PLANET_ORBIT_MAX`, with at least one planet;
     - its floors are `nominalRadii` scaled by min(1, 0.92 · a_crit / the last floor);
     - each planet's eccentricity gains e_F = 5/4 (a / a_B) e_B / (1 − e_B²), capped at `BIN.eMax`;
     - its precession period is divided by 1 + 2 (r / a_crit)³;
     - if either star cannot keep one planet, a_B × 1.03 and repeat.
  5. **The orbit.**
     - Each star orbits a new barycentre row (`host`), with a = share · a_B, e_B, ω (B: ω + π),
       period = `BIN.kep` · a_B^1.5, the pair's phase, tilt 0, and precession 0.
     - Star A takes share m_B / M and B takes m_A / M, so the heavier star makes the smaller ellipse.
     - The barycentre takes the unit's spot.
  6. **The unit's outer radius** is max over its stars of share · a_B · (1 + e) + that star's outer.
  7. **Final placement.** `placeStars` places singles and pairs as units, at 1d's `HOOD_SPACING` 1.5
     × (outer_u + outer_v) + `HOOD_MARGIN`, the scripted stars first, nearest the middle.
     - Every seed in the seed tests places. If one throws, widen `HOOD_R_WIDE` and report it.

  **Motion.** Each star is a pure function of the ambient. Its orbit about the barycentre is a Kepler
  ellipse through the same `orbitLocal` as the planets (`keplerE`, `orbitTurns`). Its planets and
  moons ride it through the host chain, which is three hops: moon → planet → star → barycentre. The
  periods run 620–790 s, so the hourly wrap jumps as the orbits' does (accepted).

  **What reads the spots:**
  - a pair is one system everywhere a system is counted: `FULL_DETAIL_MAX`, the LOD sizing
    (`uSysPx`), the clearance twins, the schematic's slots;
  - streams that end at a binary star end at it as it moves (`bodyWorld`);
  - the dissolve pulls one background star per unit, onto the barycentre, so from afar a pair reads as
    one star. Each star's real form fades in on its own glow.
